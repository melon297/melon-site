Why a job board?

At Melon 29:7, we believe you can't build a business — or anything lasting — while trying to figure out a steady source of income, unless you have savings or a strong network to support you. For a lot of the people we serve, that pressure shows up as income instability. A steady job isn't a consolation prize. It's often the first real foundation everything else gets built on.
